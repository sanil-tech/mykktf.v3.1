# MyKKTF Data Breach Response & Incident Escalation Protocol

**Document Version**: 1.0  
**Effective Date**: September 2026  
**System**: MyKKTF — Kolej Kediaman Tun Fuad, Universiti Malaysia Sabah  
**Regulatory Alignment**: Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 (Mandatory Breach Notification provisions)  

---

## 1. Incident Lifecycle Workflow

```
[ DETECTED ] ──> [ CONTAINED ] ──> [ UNDER_INVESTIGATION ] ──> [ ESCALATED ] ──> [ REMEDIATED ] ──> [ CLOSED ]
```

1. **DETECTED**: Incident identified via anomaly detection, audit log review, or staff/student report.
2. **CONTAINED**: Immediate technical containment (e.g. revoking compromised sessions, isolating endpoints).
3. **UNDER_INVESTIGATION**: Forensic review to determine scope, affected records, and sensitivity classification.
4. **ESCALATED**: Executive escalation to Pengetua KKTF, UMS Data Protection Officer (DPO), and Jabatan Digital UMS.
5. **REMEDIATED**: System patches, credential rotations, and process enhancements deployed.
6. **CLOSED**: Formal incident post-mortem signed off by university authorities.

---

## 2. Notification Policy & Institutional Safeguards

- **No Automatic External Notification from Code**: The application DOES NOT automatically dispatch automated breach notices to external regulators (JPDP) or the public.
- **Escalation Protocol**: High or Critical risk incidents trigger internal escalation workflows to enable designated UMS Legal, Executive, and Jabatan Digital officers to assess statutory reporting obligations under Malaysian law.
